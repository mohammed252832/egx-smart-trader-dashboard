## Strength score

The strength score is a transparent weighted sum, not a model probability:

- Probability: `65.0`
- Reward/risk: `20.0`
- Momentum: `10.0`
- Volume confirmation: `5.0`

Each component is normalised to 0–1, multiplied by its weight, divided by the total weight, and shown as 0–100. Reward/risk is capped at `3.00` for scoring.

## Risk rules

- Target: `max(1.5%, 1.8 × ATR%)`, capped at `9.0%` and `8.0%` upside.
- Stop: `clamp(1.1 × ATR%, 0.8%, 5.0%)`, widened to the recent support level when configured.
- EGX daily limit: `10.0%` with a `0.5%` safety buffer; prices are rounded to configured tick sizes.

## Signal thresholds

- Strong buy: probability >= `0.68`, score >= `75.0`, reward/risk >= `1.2`.
- Buy: probability >= `0.56` and reward/risk >= `1.2`.
- Avoid: probability < `0.4` or reward/risk < `1.2`.
- Watch: everything else.

All thresholds live in `engine/config/signals.json` and can be changed without code edits.
